# Griffin Fund, iOS

The phone is not the terminal and must not pretend to be.

Bloomberg's own mobile app does not ship Launchpad. It ships news,
Instant Bloomberg, market data, worksheets and alerts, and leaves the
dense multi-panel grid on the desk. The saved view travels; the layout
does not. Single-purpose tools survive as tools; whole functions do not.
The same logic governs here: a six-inch screen holds a reader, an alerter
and a messenger, and anything with columns belongs on the Mac.

## What is here

Two tabs, chosen because they are the two that work on a phone.

**Today** is the one Bloomberg does not have and the club needs most.
Their mobile alerts are market events; ours are obligations. It walks the
open research projects and collects everything the chase clock says is
actionable, rendering the server's own recommendation sentence rather
than inventing a second rule that could disagree with the Mac.

**Book** is the portfolio: the marked total and the holdings with the
day's move.

## What is deliberately absent

The outreach table, drafting, compliance screening and copy-to-send.
Those are desk work. The phone tells you Kanter replied; answering him
happens where there is a keyboard.

## Building

No XcodeGen or brew on the build machine, so `project.pbxproj` is
generated by the script in the commit that introduced it, from a fixed
file list with ids derived from each path. Regenerating never churns the
diff, and a missing tool cannot block the build.

    xcodebuild -project ios/GriffinFund.xcodeproj -target GriffinFund \
      -sdk iphonesimulator -configuration Debug \
      CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO build

Running it needs a simulator runtime (`xcodebuild -downloadPlatform iOS`)
or a signed device build. TestFlight needs the paid membership on team
PW2VT56789, not just the Developer ID used for Mac notarization.

## Rules carried over from the Mac client

- Decodables are written from the server handler, never from the JSX,
  and every field is optional: the app must render something useful
  against a payload that gained or lost a key.
- **Only a 401 ends a session.** A 429, or a 502 while Render wakes the
  dyno, keeps the login. That rule is the fix for the longest-running
  bug in this codebase and it is not to be relaxed here.
- The token lives in the keychain, never UserDefaults. It grants
  President-level access to the whole book, and a phone is lost far more
  often than a laptop.
- An empty list is good news and reads as good news, never as a failure
  to load.
